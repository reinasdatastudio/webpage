About Reina’s Data Studio

I’m Reina, the founder of Reina’s Data Studio.

I help researchers, students, technical teams, healthcare professionals and many others work with data clearly, ethically, and confidently using R.

Why I started Reina’s Data Studio

I have always wanted to build a career using R — a language I genuinely enjoy and believe is incredibly powerful, especially for healthcare and research. In reality, R-focused roles are still relatively uncommon, and I reached a point where continuing to search for another traditional role felt limiting rather than motivating.

I also realised that I work best when I can focus on helping others, working independently, and sharing what I know — rather than working under rigid structures that don’t align with how I do my best work.

Starting Reina’s Data Studio gave me the freedom to do exactly that:
to support people working with real data, and to show how effective and enjoyable R can be when used thoughtfully.

My background

My professional journey began in pharmacy, where accuracy, responsibility, and understanding the meaning behind data are essential. Over time, I realised that what I enjoyed most was analysing information and solving problems — which led me to computer science and data science.

I studied computer science with a data science focus, where R became my primary tool. I later worked in system engineering to gain broader technical experience, but my long-term goal was always to work directly with data.

Resigning from my system engineering role became a turning point — one that pushed me to fully commit to building a data-focused career on my own terms. Reina’s Data Studio is the result of that decision.

How my healthcare background shapes my work

Coming from pharmacy means I don’t treat data as abstract numbers.

I understand:

  • Where healthcare and research data comes from
  • What each variable represents
  • Why accuracy, consistency, and assumptions matter

This allows me to approach analysis with precision and context, not just technical skill. It also means I’m especially careful about ethics, privacy, and reproducibility — all of which are critical when working with sensitive data.

How I work with clients

Clients often describe my working style as:

  • Calm and detail-oriented
  • Collaborative and supportive
  • Clear and honest

I’m not afraid to ask questions — because understanding your goals properly is the fastest way to deliver work you can trust. I adapt to each client’s working style and aim for 100% clarity and satisfaction, whether that means detailed explanations or straightforward results.

Trust is central to everything I do. I want clients to feel safe sharing their data and confident in the work we produce together.

Values that guide my work

  • Transparency — clear communication, no unnecessary complexity
  • Passion for data — curiosity and care in every project
  • Education & continuous learning — knowledge grows, and so should we

These values shape both my consulting work and my teaching.

Teaching and knowledge sharing

Learning has always been a core part of my life. I truly believe that knowledge never betrays, and that learning should feel empowering — even fun.

Through YouTube tutorials, mentoring, and teaching, I share practical R knowledge so others can work more confidently with data. R is constantly evolving, and I enjoy growing alongside it while helping others do the same.

Language support

I provide support in English and Japanese.

Because many R resources are only available in English, I especially enjoy helping Japanese speakers who may be struggling with language barriers before they even get to the programming itself.

Who I work best with

I work best with people who value:

  • Mutual respect
  • Collaboration
  • Thoughtful, ethical data work

Projects that involve disrespectful communication or a lack of cooperation are not a good fit for my approach.

Let’s work together

If you have a dataset, question, or project in mind, I’d love to hear from you.
You don’t need to have everything figured out — we can work through it together.
